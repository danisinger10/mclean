FAQ

Rockville’s Top Questions, Answered

Do I need my own equipment?

Nope! We bring adjustable dumbbells, kettlebells, resistance bands, and core tools. If you have equipment, we’ll integrate it.

What does pricing look like?

Programs vary based on session frequency and coaching needs. Most Rockville clients invest $99–$139 per session, billed monthly with flexible pause options.

How soon can we start?

We typically schedule your kickoff within 5–7 days of your consultation. Early morning and evening spots fill fastest—book soon to lock yours in.

Do you work with beginners or injuries?

Yes. Our trainers are certified in corrective exercise and functional strength. We coordinate with your PT or healthcare provider to keep you safe.
