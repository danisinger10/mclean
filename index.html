Ocean City Recess is for the neighbors who make the town run

We heard from parents, service workers, and students that traditional gyms feel expensive and unwelcoming. Ocean City Recess flips the script with outdoor, coach-led sessions that cost less than a coffee and keep Mondays free for everyone.

Our goal is simple: remove cost and confidence barriers so every block in Ocean City can move together. Show up as you are—we'll supply equipment, music, and a coach who knows your name.

Questions We Hear a Lot

Do I need to be fit already?

Not at all. Coaches give low-impact and high-energy options at every station so beginners, grandparents, and teens can all join.

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes, a water bottle, and a light jacket for ocean breezes. We supply mats, bands, cones, and music.

Where do we meet?

We meet near the Ocean City boardwalk recreation lawn. Exact pin and rain plan are shared when you RSVP.

Can I sponsor a neighbor?

Yes! Let us know in the form and we will quietly cover the cost for someone who needs it on Wednesdays or Fridays.

Mondays are free. The community impact is priceless.
