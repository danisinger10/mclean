Movement Made Possible for Ocean City

Recess for adults, joy for every neighborhood.

Ocean City Recess is a new launch built for residents who want real fitness without high studio prices. Every Monday is completely free so anyone can try it, meet the coaches, and feel welcome.

Love the vibe? Come back on Wednesday or Friday for just $5-$10. Sessions focus on strength, mobility, and community connection—no fancy gear, no intimidation, just fresh air and a plan that works for every budget.

Bring a water bottle and a smile; we bring the music, mats, and easy-to-follow programming. If you need a chair, adaptive movement, or stroller space, we set it up for you.

Weekly Ocean City Schedule

Same time every week so you can plan and show up together.

Monday: Free Recess

  • Time: 6:00 PM at 33rd Street Beach Access
  • Focus: Playful circuits, light cardio, stretch
  • Cost: $0 for every Ocean City resident
  • Bring: Water bottle; we provide mats and bands
  • Goal: Meet the community and feel confident

Wednesday: Strength for $5

  • Time: 6:00 PM at Northside Park pavilion
  • Focus: Low-impact strength & balance blocks
  • Cost: Pay what you can, suggested $5

  • Goal: Build strength safely with neighbors

Friday: Flow & Reset

  • Time: 9:00 AM at Sunset Park

  • Goal: Head into the weekend loose and energized

What to Expect Every Class

Warm Welcome

Check in with the coach, share injuries or access needs, and choose your station.

  • Five-minute community check-in
  • Accessibility adjustments on the spot
  • Music and energy set for the group

Guided Blocks

Three to four blocks mixing strength, cardio, and mobility with modifications for every level.

  • Timers and clear cues so you never feel lost
  • Progressions for beginners, seniors, and athletes
  • Community high-fives encouraged

Cool Down & Resources

Group stretch, breathwork, and take-home tips to keep moving between sessions.

  • Shared mobility routine you can repeat at home
  • Weekly text reminders for schedule updates
  • Invite friends to the next free Monday

Common Questions

Is Monday really free?

Yes. Monday Recess is 100% free every week for anyone in Ocean City. No catch, no required donations.

Do I need equipment or experience?

Just bring water and wear comfortable shoes. We provide mats and bands. Every move has beginner and seated options.

What if the weather is bad?

We text updates at least 2 hours before class. Rain plan moves to the pavilion at Northside Park when available.

Can I bring kids or seniors?

Absolutely. We design every class with multi-generational options and clear spacing for strollers and chairs.
