Do I need to own equipment?

No. Your trainer arrives with everything needed—free weights, kettlebells, resistance bands, TRX, and mobility tools. We also help you build a minimalist kit if you’d like to train solo between sessions.

Where do sessions take place?

Wherever you prefer: your home, condo gym, office, or outdoors. Rockville offers great park options for warm weather sessions, and we’re happy to adapt to your space.

What does the free consultation include?

You’ll hop on a 20-minute discovery call followed by an in-person visit. We assess movement, set metrics, and craft a detailed roadmap before you invest.

Is Fit2Go right if I’m recovering from an injury?

Yes. Many of our Rockville trainers hold corrective exercise and post-rehab specializations. We collaborate with your PT or physician to keep you safe.

Can I train with a partner or family member?

Absolutely. Partner and small group sessions are popular and help reduce per-person investment. Let us know in your consultation if you’d like to train together.
