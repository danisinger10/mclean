Built for Ocean City's Hardworking Residents

Movement that matches your budget, schedule, and neighborhood.

Ocean City Recess was dreamed up to make fitness feel like play again — especially for families and workers who keep our shore town running. No fancy memberships, no hidden fees, just accessible coaching and community energy.

Monday classes are always free because everyone deserves a place to move. If you love it, you can return on Wednesdays for $5 or Fridays for $10. Bring your kids, bring your friends, and meet the neighbors you haven't had time to see this season.

Sessions are designed for all levels with short, safe progressions. We set up near playgrounds and bus stops so you can drop in before or after shifts without extra travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes, a water bottle, and a light jacket if it's breezy. We provide mats, resistance bands, chairs for seated options, and sanitizer. If you have kids, feel free to bring them — we set up near playgrounds when possible.

How do payments work for Wednesday and Friday?

We keep it simple: $5 on Wednesdays, $10 on Fridays. Cash, Venmo, or Cash App on-site. If money is tight, pay what you can — we just want you moving with us.

What happens if it rains?

We'll text updates the morning of class. If weather looks rough, we shift to an indoor community space nearby or run a shorter, sheltered session. Your RSVP helps us plan the best backup.

Are beginners welcome?

Absolutely. Every move has a seated, supported, and advanced version. We cap intensity before it becomes intimidating and check in often to keep everyone safe.

Can I bring family members?

Yes! Teens and adults can join the workout. Younger kids can hang out nearby. The more neighbors, the better the energy.
