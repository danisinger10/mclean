Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Fit2Go Rockville different?

We're not a franchise. Every Fit2Go Rockville client works with one vetted coach, receives director oversight, and gets access to our custom accountability platform. We guarantee a 24-hour response time and your satisfaction with every session.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve all of Rockville including King Farm, Woodley Gardens, Twinbrook, Fallsgrove, College Gardens, and Rockville Town Square. We also travel to North Bethesda, Potomac, and Kensington with no extra fees.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Your coach brings commercial-grade dumbbells, kettlebells, resistance bands, TRX, mobility tools, and everything else required. A 6x6 space is plenty—living room, home office, patio, or condo gym.

How fast will I see results?

Most clients feel stronger and more energetic within two weeks. Visible changes typically happen within 4-6 weeks when training and nutrition protocols are followed. Monthly assessments show exactly how your body is responding.

Can we train as a couple or group?

Absolutely. Semi-private training is one of our most popular options. We customize each participant's plan and pace while keeping the session collaborative and fun. Couples, families, and neighbors love the built-in accountability.
